### Description — 1726/4000

```text
Scores without paper or math.

Qui Mène ? keeps the score sheet for your card and board games. Enter the round's scores, each game's rules do the rest, and the standings read at a glance.

THE RULES DO THE COUNTING
• 20 games ready to play: French Tarot, Belote, Rummy, Yahtzee, Pétanque, Skyjo, Flip 7, 6 Nimmt!, Mölkky, Scrabble, Rummikub, Qwixx and more.
• Skyjo's doubling, Tarot contracts, the Yahtzee sheet, Belote teams: each game applies its own rules.
• The end of the game is detected automatically, and the last round can be undone in one tap.
• Free scoring for everything else.

ONE GAME, SEVERAL PHONES
• A six-digit code or a QR code, and everyone follows the scoreboard on their own device.
• Rounds can be entered from any phone around the table, iPhone or Android.
• The game goes on even if the device that started it is switched off.

THE SCORE ON YOUR LOCK SCREEN
• Standings update on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, without reopening the app.

AND AT THE END, A PODIUM
• Final standings, highlights, the game's trend and a round-by-round table.
• A results image to share with the group.

YOUR GROUP'S MEMORY
• History filterable by game and by player, stats and per-game leaderboards.
• Profiles and friends: games played together land in everyone's history.
• iCloud sync across your iPhone and iPad.

NO ACCOUNT, NO ADS, NO TRACKING
No account to create: a nickname and an avatar are all it takes. Games stay on your devices and in your iCloud; only the sharing features go through a server, and only for as long as needed.

Available in English, French, Spanish, German and Italian.

Game names mentioned belong to their respective owners. Qui Mène ? is not affiliated with any publisher.
```
